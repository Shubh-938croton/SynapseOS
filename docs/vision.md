# 🚀 SynapseOS Product Vision & Mission

## 1. Vision
SynapseOS is an **AI-powered Cognitive Productivity Operating System** purpose-built for students, developers, and competitive programmers. It unifies study scheduling, task execution, knowledge management, focus sessions, contest tracking, distraction-free video learning, and productivity analytics into one cohesive workspace.

## 2. Mission
To eliminate cognitive friction, eliminate context-switching between fragmented tools, and provide an intelligent, unified command center that helps learners study effectively, build consistent habits, and master complex subjects.

## 3. Core Audience
- **University & High School Students:** Managing multi-subject coursework, exam dates, lecture notes, and assignments.
- **Competitive Programmers & Software Engineers:** Tracking contests (LeetCode, Codeforces, CodeChef), building coding habits, and scheduling deep work blocks.
- **Self-Directed Learners:** Studying structured topics (DSA, System Design, Machine Learning, Web Dev) through YouTube focus mode and markdown knowledge bases.

## 5. Architectural & Design Philosophy
- **Modern Obsidian Aesthetic:** Linear/Vercel inspired dark workspace designed to reduce eye fatigue during late-night study and coding sessions.
- **Single Sign-On (SSO):** Frictionless onboarding via Google OAuth and email authentication.
- **Zero Distraction:** YouTube focus mode curates learning content without recommendation rabbit holes or algorithm distractions.
- **Privacy & Ownership:** User-isolated relational data model with strict JWT Bearer token scoping.
